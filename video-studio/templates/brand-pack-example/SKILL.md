## Hard rules for this brand
- No health claims about tea (no "detox", "cures", "boosts immunity"); see compliance.md.
- Look: warm, light, botanical, full frames. Never dark-minimal.
- No em dashes on screen. English only.
- The presenter "Mira" is an invented mascot (no real person), drawn on the pose-library route.
